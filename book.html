Before you book

Good to know

A few house notes, so your appointment runs smoothly from the first part to the last braid.

  • Deposit required

    Every appointment is secured with a deposit. Latifah confirms the amount when you book.

  • Hair is provided

    Braiding hair is included on most styles. French curls, springy bohemian twists and Bobby faux locs do not include hair, and boho styles use client-provided curly hair.

  • Arrive prepped

    Come with your hair prepared as discussed when you book, so the full appointment goes into your style.

  • Text, don’t DM

    Latifah asks for texts, not Instagram or Facebook DMs. Text (804) 664-8199 to book or ask a question.

  • Off Sundays

    Open Monday to Saturday, 8 AM to 7 PM. Longer styles take 4 to 6.5 hours, so plan your day around your appointment.
