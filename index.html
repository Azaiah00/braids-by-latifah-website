Questions

Frequently asked

Is braiding hair included?

Yes, on most styles. Latifah provides the braiding hair, and the menu flags the exceptions: French curls, springy bohemian twists and Bobby faux locs do not include hair, and boho styles need client-provided curly hair.

Do I need a deposit to book?

Yes. A deposit is required to secure every appointment. Latifah confirms the amount when you book.

How do I book an appointment?

Book online through Booksy, or text (804) 664-8199. Please text instead of sending Instagram or Facebook DMs.

How much do braids cost?

Knotless smedium box braids start at $220 with hair included, box braids start at $200, and boho box braids start at $250. Cornrows start at $80 and feed-ins are $15 per braid.

What are your hours?

Monday to Saturday, 8 AM to 7 PM. The suite is closed on Sundays.

Where are you located?

In a private suite at 101 Buford Rd, Suite 203-9, North Chesterfield, VA 23235, in the Bon Air area on the south side of Richmond.

How long does a braid appointment take?

Most full braid styles take 4 to 6.5 hours. Knotless smedium is about 6 hours, box braids about 5.5 hours, and a 2-strand twist about 3.5 hours.

Can you braid short or fine hair?

Yes. Latifah braids short and fine hair, and offers lighter styles for anyone who does not like heavy braids. Text a photo of your hair if you are unsure.

Do you braid kids and men?

Yes. Kids box braids (under 12) start at $150 and kids cornrows start at $60. Men’s cornrows start at $60.

Do you do locs and crochet?

Yes: loc retwists from $60, butterfly locs, soft dreads, Bobby faux locs and crochet braids from $100.
